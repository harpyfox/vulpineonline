Character

Using Spine 2D's Skins feature and per-Skin transform constraints, differently proportioned characters could use the same rig. Except for Professor Pounce.

Kid Detective, the player character, could earn clothing items throughout the game that could be worn together to create unique costumes. Skins were used here too.

Some characters had to be seen from behind, or only from behind. A layered approach made creating back-facing animations easier.

Every costume item had to support being viewed from the back, too.

I consulted YouTube for wheelchair usage tutorials to ensure the Concerned Girl's locomotion was accurate. My algorithm is now recommending more tutorials that I watch out of curiousity, and I'm often reminded how unaccommodating our world is for wheelchair users. We really fucked up making doors require an entire free hand to open.

Environment

Remote, asynchronous contract work can feel isolating and depressing. I liked making revision requests for assets fun and humorous to remind myself to stay positive. Hopefully, artists receiving the requests felt a similar way.
